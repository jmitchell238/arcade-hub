Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

This policy covers Arcade Hub and every game it links to, all published by James Mitchell / 238 Apps at jmitchell238.github.io. The short version: the games don't collect any personal information, and nothing you do in them is sent to us.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, names, email addresses or passwords
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access
  • No in-app purchases or payment details

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the games save things like levels reached, scores, coins, unlocked items, favorite outfits and settings (sound, motion, controls) in your browser's local storage. To work offline and load quickly, they also keep a copy of their own files in your browser's cache.

This information never leaves your device and we can't see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings, or by removing the installed app.

Spoken words

Some of the children's games can read words out loud. They use your device's built-in speech feature. Depending on your browser and device, the browser may use an online voice service run by its maker (for example Google, Apple or Microsoft) to produce the speech. That is handled by your browser under its maker's privacy policy, not by the games. Turning the sound off in a game also turns off its voice.

Hosting

The games are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub receives standard technical information when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, and keeps it in server logs. We don't have access to those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

To check for updates, a game may ask its own website for its current version number, and Arcade Hub asks each game's website for its version number. These requests go to the same GitHub Pages host and contain no information about you.

Children

Several games are made for children ages 4–6. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and none of the games ask for it. We recommend that a parent or guardian sets up and supervises play for young children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
